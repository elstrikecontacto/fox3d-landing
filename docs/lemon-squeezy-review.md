## What we plan to sell

> Fox 3D is downloadable creative software for video editors and motion designers who use Adobe Premiere Pro on Windows. It is a local Premiere Pro CEP extension, not a physical product or a done-for-you service. A user imports a PNG, JPG, or WebP image, turns it into a 3D object (transparent images retain their silhouette), adjusts depth and animation, previews the result, and exports a MOV ProRes 4444 video with transparency. The resulting file can be imported or placed in a Premiere sequence. The current product has four motion styles: gentle rotation, 360-degree rotation, sway, and pulse. It was developed by us using our own interface and motion code, with third-party components used under their respective licenses and notices. FFmpeg is a separate free component used for video encoding; it is not bundled into the installer. We plan to sell it for **USD $20 as a one-time purchase**, with a license permitting use on **up to two computers simultaneously**. **[CONFIRM UPDATE/SUPPORT TERMS]**. The software requires Windows 10 or 11, Premiere Pro 24 or later with CEP and WebGL, and a compatible FFmpeg installation for export.
